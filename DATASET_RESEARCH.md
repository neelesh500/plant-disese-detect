# Plant Disease Dataset & Feature Research

This document outlines the optimal datasets and feature extraction methods for robust plant disease detection models.

## 1. Datasets (Sourced Primarily from Kaggle)

### A. New Plant Diseases Dataset (Augmented)
- **Source:** Kaggle
- **Details:** 87K+ RGB images, 38 classes (healthy & diseased leaves).
- **Use Case:** Best for baseline deep learning model training due to sufficient data augmentation.
