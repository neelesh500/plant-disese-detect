Agronomy Tool #5

🌦️ Seasonal Crop Calendar & Sowing Planner

Comprehensive agricultural sowing windows, temperature thresholds, and critical watering stages across Indian crop seasons.

Kharif (Monsoon Season)

June - November

Crops sown at the onset of southwest monsoon. Requires high moisture and warm temperatures.

Paddy / Rice: Sowing: June-July · Harvest: Oct-Nov · Critical: Panicle initiation
Corn / Maize: Sowing: June-July · Harvest: Sept-Oct · Critical: Tasseling & silking
Cotton: Sowing: May-June · Harvest: Nov-Jan · Critical: Flowering & boll formation
Soybean: Sowing: Mid-June · Harvest: Sept-Oct · Critical: Pod development
Rabi (Winter Season)

October - April

Crops sown in autumn and harvested in spring. Thrives in cool weather and bright sunny days.

Wheat (Gehu): Sowing: Nov 1-25 · Harvest: March-April · Critical: CRI (21 DAS) & booting
Mustard (Sarson): Sowing: Oct 1-20 · Harvest: Feb-March · Critical: Flowering & pod filling
Potato: Planting: Oct 15-Nov 10 · Harvest: Jan-Feb · Critical: Tuber initiation
Gram / Chickpea: Sowing: Oct-Nov · Harvest: March · Critical: Pre-flowering & pod set
Zaid (Summer Season)

March - June

Short season crops grown between Rabi harvest and Kharif monsoon with assured irrigation.

Watermelon / Muskmelon: Sowing: Feb-March · Harvest: May-June · Needs dry hot climate
Cucumber & Squash: Sowing: Feb-March · Harvest: April-May · Frequent light irrigation
Moong / Urad Dal: Sowing: March · Harvest: May · Enrich soil with root nitrogen
